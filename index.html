Dad,

Some people are handed a father. I got to choose mine — and somehow you still turned up every single time, as if the choice had already been made for you long before I made it.

You didn't have to sit through parents' evenings, or wait up for me, or worry over my exam results like they were your own. You definitely didn't have to pay for my education. But you did — quietly, consistently, without ever once making it feel like a debt I owed you.

You just did what you do best: showed up, cheered the loudest, and never let me feel like anything less than yours.

So today isn't about goals, or trophies, or anything that fits on a scoreboard. It's about the stuff underneath that — the stuff that actually counts.

Happy birthday, Dad. Number one, forever.

With all my love Elijah
